# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sound Control is for macOS users who want to lower or mute a specific noisy app without changing the global system volume. The primary context is personal use from the menu bar while other work is already in progress.

## Product Purpose

The app discovers currently audible processes, shows them in a compact native menu, and applies per-process gain only when a custom volume or mute state is needed. Success means the utility stays quiet, trustworthy, and light on system resources while giving the user clear control.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, technical. It should feel like a native macOS utility with enough visual identity to be recognizable, not like an audio player or a marketing product.

## Anti-references

Avoid blank generic app icons, neon audio visualizers, DJ or music-production imagery, cartoon speakers, busy waveforms, and decorative glass effects that reduce small-size clarity.

## Design Principles

- Make the control metaphor readable at Finder and Dock sizes.
- Keep the interface and icon native to macOS: familiar, crisp, and restrained.
- Use warmth as a focused accent, not as a full warm-tinted interface.
- Preserve trust by making every visual cue serve audio control.
- Prefer quiet precision over spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize high contrast, distinct silhouettes, and reduced visual noise. The icon and menu should remain legible for users with color vision differences and should not rely on motion for meaning.
